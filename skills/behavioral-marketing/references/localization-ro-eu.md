# Localization — Romania / EU

Applies whenever the audience is Romanian or EU-based, in any segment.

## Tone

- Romanian audiences are hype-averse: superlatives and US-style enthusiasm
  ("revolutionary", "game-changing") read as scam signals. Understate;
  prove; let exact numbers carry the claim.
- Romanian UI/copy text: real diacritics (ă, î, â, ș, ț) — never Unicode
  escapes (renders broken in browsers).
- Formal address (dumneavoastră) for B2B/regulated and older consumer
  segments; informal (tu) acceptable for devtools and young B2C — pick one
  per artifact, never mix.

## Legal / compliance (hard constraints, not style)

- **GDPR:** consent flows can't be dark-patterned — no pre-ticked boxes, no
  consent bundling, withdrawal as easy as granting (this kills several
  "Make It Easy" shortcuts; see biases.md contraindications #2). Email
  marketing needs prior consent (opt-in), and every send needs a working
  unsubscribe.
- **Prices:** consumer-facing prices include VAT (TVA, 21% standard as of
  2025-08-01) — display "cu TVA" or all-inclusive; B2B may quote net + TVA
  explicitly. Never let a bias pattern (precision, anchoring) produce a
  price display that hides VAT.
- **EU Omnibus (price reductions):** any "discount from X" must reference
  the lowest price of the prior 30 days — invented anchor prices are
  illegal, not just unethical.
- **Claims:** comparative claims against named competitors need
  substantiation; "results not typical" hedges don't shield fabricated
  testimonials (also banned by guardrails anyway).

## Practical anchors

- Real regulatory deadlines (e-Factura mandates, ISU inspection cycles,
  fiscal-year rhythms) are strong, legitimate urgency for RO B2B — use
  them sourced, with the legal reference.
- Fresh-start moments that resonate locally: 1 January, start of school
  year (September), fiscal year end.
