# Claim verification — {artifact}

Stage-5 companion for load-bearing claims: any claim that, if false, makes
the copy deceptive (numbers, comparatives, guarantees, social proof,
urgency/scarcity, compliance/legal statements).

## Acceptable proof (in strength order)

1. Authority/auditor/regulator acceptance, certification, legal text
2. Named customer case with numbers (with permission to cite)
3. Own product/analytics data (reproducible query or metric)
4. Public benchmark or third-party study (linked, current)
5. User-stated fact from the brief (recorded in research-marketing.md)

**Not proof:** assumptions ("they'll probably raise prices"), extrapolations
presented as facts, self-written testimonials, competitor claims taken at
face value, training-data "common knowledge" for anything legal or numeric.

## Verification table

| Claim (exact copy text) | Type | Proof (level 1–5 + ref) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| {"export accepted in 14 ISU inspections"} | social proof | {level 3: case log} | kept / rewritten / dropped |

## Rules

- No proof → the claim does not ship. Rewrite to what IS provable or drop.
- Proof weaker than the claim → weaken the claim to match the proof.
- Legal/compliance claims additionally need human legal sign-off — flag
  them in the copy-draft verdict.
