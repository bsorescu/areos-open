# Playbook — Lead Magnet

Prereqs: diagnostic — lead magnets serve UNAWARE/problem-aware traffic
(segment-awareness.md); if the audience is already product-aware, a demo or
trial beats a PDF. Metrics: download → qualified-lead rate; guardrail:
post-download unsubscribe (list quality).

## Design rules

- **Promise one specific outcome**, deliverable in one sitting: "the 12
  documents ISU checks first" beats "The Complete Guide to Compliance"
  (Concreteness; a checklist/template beats an ebook).
- **The magnet IS proof:** its quality is the first product experience —
  Halo Effect works both directions. A generic AI-written PDF poisons the
  list it builds.
- **Generation Effect variant:** self-assessment quiz/scorecard ("Are you
  compliant? 10 questions") converts and qualifies simultaneously — the
  score segments the list by pain level.
- **Gate honestly:** ask email + at most one qualifying field (GDPR:
  explicit consent for the follow-up sequence, not pre-ticked; the magnet
  can't be consent-bundled with marketing).
- **Delivery is send #0** of the nurture sequence (email-sequence.md):
  deliver instantly, then ONE next step matched to their awareness level —
  not a sales pitch.

## Checklist

Title promises specific outcome · consumable in one sitting · form minimal +
GDPR-clean · delivery instant · follow-up sequence exists and matches
awareness level · magnet content itself passes claim verification (it makes
factual/legal claims too).

Close with scorecard + experiment-backlog entries.
